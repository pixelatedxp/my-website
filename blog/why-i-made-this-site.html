why i made this site

24 nov 2026 · 2 min read

so i've had pixelis.dev for a while now. the old version was fine. it worked, it had my games, some music, a bunch of random stuff. but every time i opened it, it just didn't feel like me anymore. so i tore the whole thing down and started over.

i wanted a place on the internet that's actually mine. not a profile on some app where everyone's page looks the same and an algorithm decides who sees what. just a little corner where i get to pick what goes where, what colour the stickers are, and which friends make it onto the wall.

the look came from a site i really liked, chessie.cat. it felt personal and handmade, kind of like flipping through someone's scrapbook, and i wanted that feeling here too. graph paper, polaroids, tape, postcards. stuff that looks like a real person made it.

what i cared about

  • my friends are on it. the people who somehow still talk to me get a polaroid each. they earned it.
  • you can leave a postcard. write something, draw something, and once i approve it, it stays on the wall. i read every single one.
  • the games stayed. the arcade got a makeover but it's all still there.
  • the old site isn't gone. it lives at /legacy, frozen in time, if you want to see where this started.

i'm not a designer and half of this was me changing my mind at 2am. but that's kind of the point. it's mine, it's a bit messy, and i'll keep adding to it.

if you made it this far, go leave a postcard. it means a lot to me <3
